Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when my license expires?
You have a 14-day grace period with full paid features. After that, the instance returns to Community limits for new jobs only. Running jobs always complete, and all existing data stays accessible.
Can I try Professional before buying?
Contact us for a trial license. We issue 14-day trial keys that give you full Professional or Enterprise access to test with your actual workload.
Do you offer annual pricing?
Yes! Annual subscriptions get 20% off. Professional is $2,870/year (save $718), and Enterprise pricing is custom based on your needs.
Is the license per server or per organization?
Per organization. One license key works across all your DBShift instances. The job limits apply organization-wide.
What payment methods do you accept?
Credit card, ACH, wire transfer, and purchase orders for annual contracts. We use Stripe for secure payment processing.
Is my data secure?
DBShift is self-hosted - your data never leaves your infrastructure. We never see your database credentials or migration data. The license key is verified offline.
